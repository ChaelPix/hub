About me

I am in the fifth and final year of a French engineering degree, equivalent to master’s level, specializing in robotics and computer vision. I first got into programming through game development, then gradually moved toward mixed reality and robotics. I still develop games alongside my engineering work.

I build perception and control systems for mobile robots using visual servoing and ROS. In 2024, I represented France in autonomous mobile robotics at WorldSkills Lyon, an international skills competition, and received the Best of Nation award as the highest-scoring member of the French team. During a six-month research internship at CIAD, I developed a 360° visual-servoing pipeline for a mobile manipulator. This work led to a first-author paper accepted at ICPR 2026 which I presented as a poster at Lyon.

The projects below reflect that path, from games and mixed-reality prototypes to competition robots and research.
