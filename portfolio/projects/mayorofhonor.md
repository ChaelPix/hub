# Mayor of Honor

## Overview

Mayor of Honor is a top down action game made in three days for the Roblox Inspire 2026 challenge. It was my first Roblox Studio project, so I learned the engine while building the game.

I did not want the first victory to be a line of text before another level. In Mayor of Honor, winning actually changes what the game is. You spend the afternoon earning votes, become Mayor, and only then discover what the job really means.

**@KenjiCrafts** designed and built the village map. I made the rest of the game, including the gameplay code, UI, dialogue, cinematics, combat systems, sound integration and game flow.

## The election is also your loadout

The first phase takes place during the day. You meet villagers, help with their requests and try to secure three votes before 5 PM. It looks like a small election game, but it is also how the player builds their loadout.

<figure class="project-video-card project-video-wide"><video src="img/projects/mayorofhonor/day_quests.mp4" data-autoplay controls muted loop playsinline></video><figcaption>Talking to villagers, collecting resources and earning their votes</figcaption></figure>

The exact three citizens who vote for you become your three ability cards for the night. A knight, wizard or craftsman does not only change the dialogue. Each one gives the player a different combat ability. This made the day phase matter once the combat started.

## Victory starts the real job

After the election, the game congratulates you. Then the rival candidate explains that the village gets dangerous after dark. The colorful afternoon moves into sunset, the villagers draw their weapons, and the camera returns to the same village at night.

<figure class="project-video-card project-video-wide"><video src="img/projects/mayorofhonor/mayor_duty.mp4" data-autoplay controls muted loop playsinline></video><figcaption>Winning the election reveals the Mayor's real duty</figcaption></figure>

This was the most important part of the theme for me. Becoming Mayor is a real victory, but it also gives the player new abilities and responsibility for everyone who voted for them.

## Killing everything is not enough

The night phase is a top down wave shooter, but survival alone does not clear a wave. Every attack can build Style and Multiplier, which are converted into Village Honor. Close range kills, critical hits, citizen abilities and avoiding damage improve the score. If the last enemy dies before the Honor target is reached, the village loses faith in its Mayor and the run ends.

## Decrees change each wave

Between waves, the player spends coins on Mayor's Decrees. These can change scoring rules, strengthen abilities or make survival easier. Every third wave also introduces a Village Crisis with a new penalty, so finishing one wave immediately creates a new decision for the next.

## Building it in the jam

On Friday, I had never made a Roblox Studio project. By Sunday, the game had a menu, NPC quests, resource collection, cinematics, a personalized ability loadout, server controlled combat, score based waves, Decree shops, crisis modifiers, game over states and a full return to the lobby.
